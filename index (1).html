012026 · Team of 6

MineSight

Hazard detection and route planning for mine vehicles in fog. Smart India Hackathon 2026 Grand Finalist. I led the algorithm pipeline.

  • Designed the algorithm pipeline: radar, thermal and ultrasonic data are fused, an AI model picks out people, vehicles, road edges and mud or water, and the output becomes a live hazard map around the vehicle.
  • Built hazard sharing between vehicles over V2V and mesh links, so a hazard one vehicle sees shows up on the maps of nearby vehicles.
  • Used D* Lite to re-plan routes when road segments get new weights for visibility, soil moisture and traffic. Updated routes and alerts go to the driver's in-cab screen.
  • Tested on an Ackermann-steering rover.
